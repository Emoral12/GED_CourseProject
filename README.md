Welcome to Time Cop!

We have made a simple beat 'em up game where you can move left and right with A and D. You can also jump with the space bar, and rewind time with R, and attack with left click. We also have plans for remappable controls using command. 

You play as a time cop, beating up and arresting people and creatures who step out of the space in time they are supposed to exist in. The goal of the game is to defeat as many of these time anomalies as you can before they defeat you to reach the highest score you can! You'll have an HP bar to show off how injured you are so make sure to plan your moves out wisely!
